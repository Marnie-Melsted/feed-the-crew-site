Everything we get asked before someone signs up. Don't see yours? Ask us directly.

Getting started

What is Feed the Crew?
A meal-planning tool for anyone feeding a large group — a family gathering, a sports team, a camp, a retreat, or a catering job. You pick a menu, and it builds a scaled grocery list, tracks cost, and manages dietary needs automatically.
Who is this for?
Anyone planning food for more people than a normal household — from a fifteen-person family reunion to a two-hundred-person catering event. Coaches, camp directors, retreat organizers, and professional caterers are our most common Pro users; families and community organizers are our most common Home & Family users.
How does the free demo work?
You can watch a full walkthrough on our home page before signing up. We don't offer a hands-on free trial — every account starts on a paid plan — because the tool's real value only shows up once you're planning a real group's real week, and we'd rather you see exactly what you're getting than poke at a limited sandbox version.
What's the difference between Home & Family and Pro & Catering?
Home & Family covers everything most families and small groups need: menu planning, grocery lists, live costing, and dietary swaps, for groups up to 20 and plans up to 3 days. Pro & Catering removes those caps, adds AI-powered tools (photo receipt scanning, AI nutrition estimates for custom recipes), lets you keep multiple saved plans at once, and includes phone support. See the full comparison.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from your account page any time — you'll keep access through the end of your current billing period, no long-term contract.

The AI features

How accurate is the AI receipt scanner?
It reads item names, quantities, and prices from a photo and matches them to your grocery list automatically. It's a time-saver, not a guarantee — always spot-check the totals against your actual receipt, especially for items it couldn't confidently match.
How accurate are the AI nutrition estimates?
They're a planning estimate for custom recipes you add, generated from the ingredient list — not lab-tested nutrition data. For medical or strict dietary needs, verify with a registered dietitian or the actual product labels.
Is my data private? What happens to a photo I upload?
Receipt and invoice photos are sent securely to our AI provider only to extract item and price data, and are not stored longer than needed to process your request. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

Dietary needs & recipes

Do you support gluten-free, dairy-free, or other dietary needs?
Gluten-free and dairy-free swaps are built in and automatically adjust your grocery list. Other allergens (nuts, shellfish, egg) are flagged on affected recipes so you can plan around them, though we don't yet offer automatic substitutions for those.
Can I add my own recipes?
Yes, on both plans. Add ingredients, method, and nutrition info manually; Pro accounts can also use the AI nutrition estimator to help fill in calories and protein.
Will the grocery costs match my local store exactly?
No tool can guarantee that — prices vary by region, store, and season. You enter your own prices (or scan a receipt) so the numbers reflect what you actually pay, not a national average.
