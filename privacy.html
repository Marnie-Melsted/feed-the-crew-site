Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Account information: name, email, password (encrypted), billing details (processed by our payment provider — we don't store your card number ourselves).
  • Plan data: group size, dates, dietary needs, recipes you add, grocery prices you enter.
  • Uploaded photos: receipts or invoices you scan using the AI receipt scanner.
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, general analytics.

How we use it

To run the service you signed up for, process payments and manage your subscription, improve the product using aggregated and anonymized usage patterns, and respond to support requests.

Receipt & invoice photos specifically

Photos you upload for the AI receipt scanner are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) solely to extract item names, quantities, and prices. We do not use these photos to train any AI model, and we do not retain them longer than needed to process your request.

Third parties we share data with

  • [Your hosting provider] — hosting and infrastructure.
  • [Your payment processor] — payment processing.
  • Anthropic — processes receipt photos and custom recipe ingredient lists to power AI features, only when you use those features.

We do not sell your data to advertisers or third parties.
